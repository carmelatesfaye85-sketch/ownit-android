# OwnIt for Android

OwnIt stops TikTok, Instagram and YouTube from opening unless you go through OwnIt first.
You say what you're looking for and how long you need. When time's up, OwnIt covers the app with a
full-screen "Time's up" screen, buzzes, and brings you back.

## Download
Get **OwnIt.apk** from the **Releases** section of this page (right side), then open it on your phone.

## Setup on the phone (one time)
1. Open **OwnIt.apk**. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source, then tap **Install**.
2. Open OwnIt and sign in.
3. Tap **Turn on** on the "Turn on OwnIt Guard" card. Find **OwnIt Guard** (sometimes under
   "Installed apps" or "Downloaded apps") and switch it on.
4. **If the switch is greyed out** (Android 13 and newer): go to **Settings → Apps → OwnIt**, tap the
   **⋮** menu at the top right, tap **Allow restricted settings**, then do step 3 again.

## How it's built
Every change pushed to this repository is built for free by GitHub Actions
(`.github/workflows/build.yml`), and the new OwnIt.apk is published under Releases.
